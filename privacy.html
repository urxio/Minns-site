Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Minns is built so that your reminders stay yours. In short: we don't collect, store or share any of your data. Minns has no account, no servers and no analytics or advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

Your reminders, notes, settings, the name you may enter for the greeting, the places you save (such as Home and Work) and everything in your brain dump, including photos and files you add, are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Because they are part of the app's data on your phone, they are included in your own iCloud or computer device backups if you have those turned on, and they are restored with them. That backup is between you and Apple.

Brain dump

The brain dump keeps thoughts, links, photos and files you add, whether you type them in Minns, add them with the + button or share them to Minns from another app. Minns sorts them into categories on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device model where available and built-in rules otherwise. Photos and files are copied into Minns' own storage on your iPhone; they aren't uploaded anywhere.

When you save a link, Minns asks that website for the page's title and preview image, the same way your browser would if you opened it, so the site sees an ordinary request from your iPhone. Minns sends it nothing else, and nothing about it reaches us.

Backup files (optional)

If you choose Export a backup in Settings, Minns saves your reminders and your brain dump, photos and files included, as one file wherever you pick. It goes nowhere else, and only you decide who gets it.

Permissions Minns asks for

  • Notifications: to ring when a reminder is due, and for the optional morning note about reminders still open from earlier days. Alerts are scheduled on your device and don't go through any server.
  • Location: only if you give a reminder a place, such as “when I leave work”. Minns asks when you first set a place, never just for opening the app. Your iPhone then tells Minns when you arrive at or leave the places you chose; with Always access this works while Minns is closed, and with While Using only while it's open. Minns doesn't record where you go or keep a location history; it keeps only the places you saved. Searching for a place uses Apple Maps, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can change or turn off this access at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Photos and files: Minns uses Apple's photo and file pickers, so it only receives the photos and files you choose to add. It has no access to the rest of your library or your files.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: only if you use voice entry, and only while you are adding a reminder. Minns turns what you say into text. Where your language supports it, recognition happens on your iPhone. If it doesn't, iOS may send the audio to Apple to be transcribed, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Minns does not keep recordings. Typing always works if you decline.
  • Reminders: only if you choose to import from Apple Reminders, in Settings or when Minns offers it after setup. Minns reads the unfinished reminders in the lists you pick and copies their titles, notes, due dates, repeats and priority into Minns on your iPhone. It never changes or deletes anything in Apple Reminders, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn this access off at any time in iPhone Settings.

On-device understanding

Minns understands phrases like “call Mom in 30 minutes” using Apple's on-device model when your iPhone supports it, and with built-in rules otherwise. Your text is not sent to us or to any third-party service.

Lock Screen, widgets and notifications

Notifications, Live Activities and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets are created by the app on your device. Widgets read a small copy of your upcoming reminders that Minns keeps in its own storage on your iPhone; it is never sent anywhere. Notifications and widgets can show a reminder's title and notes, and Live Activities show its title. If you turn on Hide reminder details in Settings or mark a reminder sensitive, Minns shows generic text instead and leaves the notes out.

Children

Minns collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
